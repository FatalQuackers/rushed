GOOD TO KNOW.

Do you only work on new games?

No. We explore new concepts and also help existing games take their next step.

My game has very few players. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We care about potential, not popularity. A small game with a strong idea is exactly what we look for.

Are you hiring?

We're a small team, but we always like hearing from talented people. Pick "I want to join the team" in the form and tell us what you do best.

How long until I hear back?

We read every message. If it's a fit, we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
