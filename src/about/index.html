OUR STORYWHY RUSHED EXISTS

PLAYER COUNT
ISN'T THE
WHOLE STORY.

Plenty of great ideas never get the attention they deserve.

We started RUSHED because the numbers on a game page rarely tell you what a game could become. A strong core loop, a clear idea or a team with drive can matter far more than today's player count.

So we look closer. We spend time with the idea, the people behind it and the players it's for — and then we put in the work to move it forward.

WHAT WE BELIEVEFOUR RULES WE WORK BY

OUR VALUES.

01

Potential over popularity

We judge an idea by what it could become, not by how loud it is today.

02

Players first. Always.

Every decision starts with one question: does this make the game better to play?

03

No shortcuts

We'd rather ship something solid a little later than something rushed today.

04

Small and focused

A small team means fewer meetings, faster decisions and real ownership.

HOW WE WORKFROM FIRST LOOK TO WHAT'S NEXT

THE PROCESS.

  1. 01Scout

    We play, read and ask questions to understand the idea, its audience and its room to grow.

  2. 02Shape

    We agree on what the game is really about and plan the smallest version that proves it.

  3. 03Build

    Systems, gameplay and presentation come together in short loops with lots of play-testing.

  4. 04Grow

    After launch we keep listening: updates, community and the work that keeps momentum going.
